# Chat IR marketing website — approved sitemap

Status: Approved by the user in the website design conversation.

## Audience

Public companies, particularly investor relations teams and finance leadership.

## Pages

| Page | Proposed route | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/` | Hero, chat/voice agent demo, benefits, how it works, closing demo CTA. |
| Platform | `/platform` | Chat agents, voice agents, knowledge sources, conversation analytics, product examples, and deployment details. |

Routes are proposed implementation details; preserve existing application routes when integrating the marketing site.

## Navigation

Header: Platform · For IR Teams · Pricing · Resources · Log In · **Book a Demo**

Footer: Main pages · About · Contact · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Contact can link to contact information on About; no separate Contact page is required for launch.

## Approved home page direction

1. Dark, quiet architectural hero with ivory text and emerald actions.
2. Warm ivory chat/voice demo panel, visually prominent against the hero.
3. Charcoal benefits section with deep forest-green cards and subtle borders.
4. Pale sage How it works section: connect content, launch agents, review conversations.
5. Warm ivory closing demo CTA.
6. Compact charcoal footer.

Maintain generous spacing and a restrained, credible financial-services aesthetic. The latest visual mockup is the design reference; example conversations and product illustrations remain illustrative.

## Launch boundaries

- Keep chat and voice together on Platform initially.
- Add separate product pages, customer stories, and a dedicated security page when sufficient verified content exists.
- Do not invent customer logos, testimonials, certifications, performance metrics, or product capabilities.
- Subsequently approved pricing: $99/month with 14 days free. No unverified usage allowances are advertised.
- Sitemap approval does not constitute approval of unreviewed legal or commercial copy.
